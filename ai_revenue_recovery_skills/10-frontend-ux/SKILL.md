# Skill: Frontend UX & Operator Experience

## Mission
Make recovery operations understandable and trustworthy.

## Principle
Show business outcome first, system complexity second.

Top dashboard metrics:
- Revenue at Risk
- Revenue Recovered
- Recovery Rate
- Cases Processed
- Escalations
- Policy Blocks

## Case detail must answer
- What happened?
- Why is revenue at risk?
- What did the system conclude?
- What action was recommended?
- Why?
- What policy allowed or blocked it?
- What happened afterward?
- What can the human do now?

## UX rules
- Do not hide uncertainty.
- Clearly label AI recommendation vs executed action.
- Show current state and next possible state.
- Make terminal/blocked conditions obvious.
- Prevent accidental high-impact actions.
- Provide audit timelines for important cases.
- Avoid dashboards overloaded with decorative charts.

## Human override
If allowed, make:
- permission
- reason
- effect
- audit consequence
explicit.

## States
Represent loading, empty, error, stale, blocked, escalated, and recovered states. Do not design only success states.
