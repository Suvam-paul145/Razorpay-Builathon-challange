# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Revora is an AI-assisted incremental revenue recovery decision system. Revora detects revenue at risk, diagnoses why the revenue is at risk, estimates what happens with no intervention, simulates candidate recovery interventions, selects the intervention with the highest safe incremental recovery value, validates that selection through a deterministic policy layer, executes a bounded action, verifies the payment outcome against authoritative payment data, and measures revenue actually recovered.

Revora is not a payment reminder service, a chatbot, a voice agent, a retry engine, a dunning platform, a collection bot, or a multi-agent demonstration. The decision question Revora answers is not "which customer is likely to pay" but "which intervention is likely to make a meaningful economic difference." A customer with a high natural recovery probability may warrant no intervention at all.

**Core principle: AI recommendation is not AI authority.** The Reasoning_Layer may analyze and recommend. Deterministic components validate and authorize. External payment or communication actions occur only after Policy_Engine approval.

**Primary business metric:** rupees of revenue recovered.
**Strategic metric:** rupees of *incremental* revenue recovered, evidenced by controlled comparison.

This document covers MVP Phase 1 scope only. Deferred capabilities are listed in "Scope Boundaries" so that exclusions are explicit rather than silent.

### Evidence Discipline Used In This Document

Requirements that rest on unverified premises carry an inline tag:

- **[FACT]** — verifiable from the project brief, from established engineering practice, or from data already in hand.
- **[ASSUMPTION]** — a working premise chosen to make the requirement testable; must be confirmed or replaced during design.
- **[INFERENCE]** — a conclusion derived from stated premises rather than direct evidence.
- **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** — the value, capability, or external behavior is unknown at requirements time and must be verified before design closes. No invented API names, endpoint shapes, regulatory claims, or industry recovery-rate figures appear in this document.

Payment provider capabilities are referenced generically as the Payment_Provider throughout. Specific API surfaces, webhook event names, payload shapes, signature schemes, and recovery-action availability are tagged **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** and deferred to design-phase verification against live provider documentation.

### Positioning Note

Revora's proposed differentiation is economic decision quality — selecting actions by expected net incremental value and proving that value experimentally. Intelligent retry, dunning sequencing, and communication automation already exist in the market; Revora claims no uniqueness in action automation. The gap under investigation is whether incremental-value optimization produces more recovered revenue than a baseline recovery workflow at equal or lower operational and customer cost. **[INFERENCE]** — competitor internals were not audited.

## Glossary

### Systems and Components

- **Revora**: The complete system described by this document, deployed as a modular monolith.
- **Event_Ingestion**: The component that receives, authenticates, deduplicates, and persists inbound Payment_Events.
- **Detection_Engine**: The deterministic component that classifies persisted Payment_Events as revenue at risk and opens Recovery_Cases.
- **Recovery_Case_Manager**: The component that owns Recovery_Case state and enforces legal state transitions and lifecycle bounds.
- **Diagnosis_Engine**: The component that determines the Risk_Cause of a Recovery_Case.
- **Baseline_Model**: The component that estimates baseline_recovery_probability.
- **Intervention_Simulator**: The component that estimates outcome and cost figures for each Candidate_Action.
- **Value_Optimizer**: The component that computes net_recovery_value per Candidate_Action and produces a Recommendation.
- **Policy_Engine**: The deterministic component holding final authority over whether any Candidate_Action may execute.
- **Execution_Engine**: The component that performs approved external actions against the Payment_Provider or the Communication_Provider.
- **Outcome_Monitor**: The component that observes payment state changes and resolves Recovery_Case outcomes.
- **Experiment_Engine**: The component that assigns Recovery_Cases to Control_Group or Treatment_Group and computes experiment results.
- **Audit_Log**: The append-only store of Audit_Records.
- **Metrics_Engine**: The component that computes recovery metrics from Recovery_Cases, Audit_Records, and verified payment state.
- **Recovery_Memory**: The store of historical Recovery_Case features and outcomes used by Baseline_Model and Intervention_Simulator.
- **Merchant_Dashboard**: The web interface presenting Recovery_Cases, decisions, policy outcomes, and metrics to the Merchant.
- **Reasoning_Layer**: The large-language-model-backed component used for ambiguous interpretation, explanation, and message drafting.
- **Payment_Provider**: The external payment platform that emits payment events and exposes payment state and recovery actions (Razorpay for the MVP). **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** on specific capabilities.
- **Communication_Provider**: The external service used to deliver a Customer_Message. **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** on selection.

### Domain Terms

- **Merchant**: The business that owns the at-risk revenue and operates Revora.
- **Merchant_User**: An authenticated human operating the Merchant_Dashboard on behalf of one Merchant.
- **Payment_Event**: A structured inbound record describing a payment or checkout state change.
- **Recovery_Case**: The unit of work tracking one at-risk payment from detection to a Terminal_State.
- **Risk_Cause**: The classified reason revenue is at risk, drawn from the enumeration: INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS, EXPIRED_PAYMENT_METHOD, BANK_OR_NETWORK_FAILURE, TECHNICAL_ISSUE, ABANDONMENT, CUSTOMER_ACTION_REQUIRED, FRAUD_OR_RISK_SIGNAL, UNKNOWN.
- **Candidate_Action**: One member of the allowed action set: DO_NOTHING, WAIT, RETRY, DELAYED_RETRY, PAYMENT_LINK, CUSTOMER_MESSAGE, PAYMENT_METHOD_UPDATE, PROMISE_TO_PAY_FOLLOW_UP, HUMAN_ESCALATION.
- **payment_amount**: The at-risk amount A of a Recovery_Case, in minor currency units.
- **baseline_recovery_probability**: The estimated probability that the payment recovers with no Revora intervention.
- **intervention_recovery_probability**: The estimated probability that the payment recovers when a given Candidate_Action executes.
- **incremental_probability**: intervention_recovery_probability minus baseline_recovery_probability.
- **expected_incremental_revenue**: payment_amount multiplied by incremental_probability.
- **action_cost**: The direct monetary cost of executing a Candidate_Action.
- **risk_cost**: The monetized expected cost of risk exposure created by a Candidate_Action.
- **customer_cost**: The monetized expected cost of customer friction and operational handling created by a Candidate_Action.
- **net_recovery_value**: expected_incremental_revenue minus action_cost minus risk_cost minus customer_cost.
- **Recommendation**: The Value_Optimizer output naming one selected Candidate_Action, the rejected alternatives, and the value figures behind the selection.
- **Policy_Decision**: The Policy_Engine verdict for a Recommendation, one of APPROVED, BLOCKED, DEFERRED, ESCALATE.
- **Idempotency_Key**: The deterministic identifier that makes one logical external action execute at most once.
- **Recovery_Window**: The bounded time interval during which Revora may act on a Recovery_Case.
- **Cooldown**: The minimum interval between two consecutive outbound actions on one Recovery_Case.
- **Terminal_State**: One of RECOVERED, STOPPED, BLOCKED, EXPIRED, ESCALATED, FAILED.
- **Natural_Recovery**: A recovery that completed with no Revora intervention on that Recovery_Case.
- **Observed_Recovery**: A recovery that completed after a Revora intervention, with causality not established.
- **Attributed_Recovery**: A recovery increment supported by controlled Control_Group versus Treatment_Group comparison.
- **Control_Group**: Recovery_Cases handled by the Baseline_Workflow.
- **Treatment_Group**: Recovery_Cases handled by Revora decisioning.
- **Baseline_Workflow**: The Merchant's pre-Revora recovery behavior, reproduced deterministically for comparison.
- **Synthetic_Dataset**: Generated data used where Merchant production data is unavailable.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Payment Event Ingestion and Failed Payment Detection

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want Revora to reliably receive payment events from the Payment_Provider and open a recovery case for each genuinely at-risk payment, so that no recoverable revenue is missed and no payment is tracked twice.

MVP detection priority is failed payment recovery. Checkout abandonment, missed promise-to-pay, and payment-window expiry are modelled in the event schema but are deferred as detection triggers (see Scope Boundaries). **[FACT]** from project scope. Provider webhook event names, signature algorithm, and payload fields are **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** and must be verified in design.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN Event_Ingestion receives an inbound Payment_Event, THE Event_Ingestion SHALL verify the Payment_Provider signature over the unmodified raw request body before interpreting the payload contents, and SHALL treat an absent or unreadable signature as a verification failure.
2. IF Payment_Provider signature verification fails or the inbound Payment_Event carries no signature, THEN THE Event_Ingestion SHALL reject the Payment_Event, respond with HTTP status 401, retain no copy of the payload in the event store, create no Recovery_Case, and write a SIGNATURE_REJECTED Audit_Record carrying the receipt timestamp and the rejection reason without recording signature secret material.
3. WHEN Event_Ingestion accepts a signature-verified Payment_Event, THE Event_Ingestion SHALL durably persist the raw payload together with the provider_event_id, the receipt timestamp, and the provider timestamp before any downstream component processes the Payment_Event.
4. WHEN Event_Ingestion receives a Payment_Event whose provider_event_id matches a provider_event_id already persisted within EVENT_DEDUP_RETENTION **[ASSUMPTION]**, THE Event_Ingestion SHALL discard the duplicate, create no additional Recovery_Case, respond with HTTP status 200, and write a DUPLICATE_EVENT_DISCARDED Audit_Record naming the retained provider_event_id; THE Event_Ingestion SHALL perform the uniqueness check and the persistence of criterion 3 as one atomic operation so that concurrent deliveries carrying one provider_event_id yield exactly one persisted Payment_Event.
5. WHEN Event_Ingestion has persisted a Payment_Event, THE Event_Ingestion SHALL respond to the Payment_Provider within INGEST_ACK_TIMEOUT measured from receipt of the complete inbound request to transmission of the response, and SHALL complete signature verification, deduplication, and persistence within that interval without waiting for Detection_Engine evaluation.
6. IF a Payment_Event fails schema validation or the inbound payload exceeds MAX_INBOUND_PAYLOAD_SIZE **[ASSUMPTION]**, THEN THE Event_Ingestion SHALL store the payload in the quarantine store for QUARANTINE_RETENTION **[ASSUMPTION]**, create no Recovery_Case, respond with HTTP status 202, and write a MALFORMED_EVENT Audit_Record naming the validation rule that failed.
7. WHEN Event_Ingestion persists a Payment_Event carrying a provider timestamp earlier than the newest already-processed provider timestamp for the same payment identifier, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL apply the Payment_Event only where the Payment_Event produces a legal forward state transition, SHALL otherwise leave the Recovery_Case state and every Recovery_Case counter unchanged, and SHALL write an OUT_OF_ORDER_EVENT Audit_Record recording the two compared provider timestamps and whether the transition was applied.
8. WHEN Detection_Engine evaluates a persisted failed-payment Payment_Event, THE Detection_Engine SHALL classify the Payment_Event as revenue at risk using deterministic rules over structured payment fields, classifying as revenue at risk only where the reported payment state is a failed state, payment_amount is greater than or equal to MIN_DETECTION_AMOUNT **[ASSUMPTION]**, currency is a member of the configured supported currency set, and no verified paid or captured state exists for the payment identifier, and SHALL record the identifier of every rule applied.
9. WHEN Detection_Engine classifies a Payment_Event as revenue at risk and no Recovery_Case in a non-Terminal_State exists for the payment identifier, THE Detection_Engine SHALL create exactly one Recovery_Case holding payment_amount, currency, customer identifier, provider payment identifier, and detection timestamp, SHALL make that creation atomic on the payment identifier so that concurrent evaluations produce exactly one Recovery_Case, and SHALL write an Audit_Record naming the created Recovery_Case identifier and the applied detection rule identifiers.
10. WHERE a Recovery_Case in a non-Terminal_State already exists for the payment identifier, THE Detection_Engine SHALL attach the new Payment_Event to that existing Recovery_Case, SHALL create no second Recovery_Case, and SHALL leave the existing payment_amount and detection timestamp of that Recovery_Case unchanged.
11. IF a Payment_Event reports a payment state of paid or captured for a payment identifier holding a Recovery_Case in a non-Terminal_State, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL suppress further action evaluation and action scheduling for that Recovery_Case, SHALL mark the Recovery_Case for outcome resolution against verified payment state before any Candidate_Action is evaluated, and SHALL record the reported payment state together with the identifier of the reporting Payment_Event.
12. THE Detection_Engine SHALL reach a detection verdict without invoking the Reasoning_Layer.
13. IF durable persistence of a signature-verified Payment_Event does not complete, THEN THE Event_Ingestion SHALL create no Recovery_Case, SHALL respond with a failure status that permits Payment_Provider redelivery, and SHALL write an Audit_Record carrying the receipt timestamp, the provider_event_id where available, and the persistence failure indication. Payment_Provider redelivery behavior is **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** and must be verified in design.
14. WHEN Event_Ingestion has persisted a signature-verified, schema-valid Payment_Event, THE Detection_Engine SHALL record for that Payment_Event exactly one detection verdict drawn from AT_RISK, NOT_AT_RISK, and DEFERRED_TRIGGER within DETECTION_LATENCY_BOUND **[ASSUMPTION]** measured from persistence.
15. WHERE a persisted Payment_Event describes checkout abandonment, missed promise-to-pay, or payment-window expiry, THE Detection_Engine SHALL record the detection verdict DEFERRED_TRIGGER, SHALL create no Recovery_Case, and SHALL retain the Payment_Event for evaluation after the deferred trigger enters scope. **[FACT]** MVP scope covers failed-payment detection only.

### Requirement 2: Recovery Case Lifecycle, State Legality, and Boundedness

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want every recovery case to follow one legal, bounded lifecycle that always terminates, so that no customer is pursued indefinitely and every case has an explainable end state.

Defined lifecycle: NEW → DETECTED → DIAGNOSED → DECISION_PENDING → POLICY_CHECK → ACTION_SCHEDULED → EXECUTING → WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME → RECOVERED, with Terminal_State alternatives STOPPED, BLOCKED, EXPIRED, ESCALATED, FAILED. **[FACT]** from project scope.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL persist for each Recovery_Case a current state drawn from the fourteen-member lifecycle enumeration NEW, DETECTED, DIAGNOSED, DECISION_PENDING, POLICY_CHECK, ACTION_SCHEDULED, EXECUTING, WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME, RECOVERED, STOPPED, BLOCKED, EXPIRED, ESCALATED, FAILED, and SHALL set that state to NEW at Recovery_Case creation.
2. WHEN a component requests a Recovery_Case state transition, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL apply the transition only where the ordered pair of prior state and new state appears in the legal transition table, which comprises exactly the forward pairs NEW→DETECTED, DETECTED→DIAGNOSED, DIAGNOSED→DECISION_PENDING, DECISION_PENDING→POLICY_CHECK, POLICY_CHECK→ACTION_SCHEDULED, ACTION_SCHEDULED→EXECUTING, EXECUTING→WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME, WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME→RECOVERED, the re-entry pair WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME→DECISION_PENDING, and one pair from each non-terminal state to each Terminal_State alternative STOPPED, BLOCKED, EXPIRED, ESCALATED, FAILED; and SHALL write an Audit_Record carrying the prior state, the new state, the transition reason, and the transition timestamp. **[INFERENCE]** — the table is derived from the defined lifecycle.
3. IF a requested Recovery_Case state transition is absent from the legal transition table of criterion 2, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL reject the request, leave the current state and every Recovery_Case counter unchanged, return a rejection indicating an illegal transition to the requesting component, and write an ILLEGAL_TRANSITION Audit_Record carrying the requested prior state and the requested new state.
4. WHEN a Recovery_Case enters a Terminal_State, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL reject every subsequent transition request for that Recovery_Case except at most one reconciliation transition to RECOVERED, applied only where the Outcome_Monitor supplies a verified Payment_Provider payment state of paid or captured, and SHALL reject every reconciliation request following that one.
5. THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL assign every Recovery_Case a Recovery_Window whose duration equals RECOVERY_WINDOW_DURATION measured from the detection timestamp, SHALL persist the resulting window end timestamp at Recovery_Case creation, and SHALL never extend or reset that window end timestamp.
6. WHEN the persisted Recovery_Window end timestamp of a Recovery_Case is reached while that Recovery_Case holds a non-terminal state, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to EXPIRED with the transition reason RECOVERY_WINDOW_ELAPSED and SHALL record the unresolved payment_amount in minor currency units.
7. THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL maintain per Recovery_Case an executed-action counter, a customer-message counter, a decision-cycle counter, and the timestamp of the most recent outbound action; SHALL initialize all three counters to zero at Recovery_Case creation; SHALL increment the executed-action counter by one and set the most-recent-outbound-action timestamp at the transition into EXECUTING before any Payment_Provider or Communication_Provider request is issued; SHALL additionally increment the customer-message counter by one where the executing action is customer-visible; SHALL increment the decision-cycle counter by one at each transition into DECISION_PENDING; and SHALL never decrement any of the three counters.
8. WHEN the executed-action counter of a Recovery_Case equals MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS and the outcome of the most recent executed action resolves to a payment state other than paid or captured, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to ESCALATED where payment_amount is greater than or equal to ESCALATION_AMOUNT_THRESHOLD, SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to STOPPED otherwise, and SHALL record the transition reason MAX_ATTEMPTS_REACHED. **[ASSUMPTION]** — the amount-threshold escalation rule is a working premise pending Merchant confirmation.
9. WHILE a Recovery_Case holds the state WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL reject requests to schedule an additional action for that Recovery_Case, leave the current state and every counter unchanged, and write a SCHEDULE_REJECTED Audit_Record carrying the reason OUTCOME_PENDING.
10. WHEN OUTCOME_WAIT_TIMEOUT elapses measured from the transition into WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME, THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL query authoritative Payment_Provider payment state for the Recovery_Case and THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to RECOVERED where the returned payment state is paid or captured, to DECISION_PENDING where the returned payment state is neither paid nor captured and all of the conditions executed-action counter below MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS, decision-cycle counter below MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS, current time earlier than the Recovery_Window end timestamp, and COOLDOWN_INTERVAL elapsed since the most-recent-outbound-action timestamp hold, and to a Terminal_State of EXPIRED where the Recovery_Window end timestamp has been reached or STOPPED where any remaining condition fails.
11. IF a component requests scheduling of an action on a Recovery_Case while any one of the conditions executed-action counter greater than or equal to MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS, requested action customer-visible with customer-message counter greater than or equal to MAX_CUSTOMER_MESSAGES, elapsed time since the most-recent-outbound-action timestamp shorter than COOLDOWN_INTERVAL, current time at or after the Recovery_Window end timestamp, or current state equal to a Terminal_State holds, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL reject the scheduling request, leave the current state and every counter unchanged, and write a SCHEDULE_REJECTED Audit_Record naming the specific bound that produced the rejection.
12. THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL cause every Recovery_Case to reach a Terminal_State no later than RECOVERY_WINDOW_DURATION plus OUTCOME_WAIT_TIMEOUT plus LIFECYCLE_EVALUATION_INTERVAL after the detection timestamp, and SHALL execute no more than MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS outbound actions and no more than MAX_CUSTOMER_MESSAGES customer-visible actions across the whole lifetime of that Recovery_Case. **[INFERENCE]** — the worst-case bound follows from criteria 5, 7, 10, 11, 13, and 14.
13. THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL evaluate every Recovery_Case holding a non-terminal state at least once per LIFECYCLE_EVALUATION_INTERVAL, and SHALL apply any Recovery_Window elapse, Cooldown elapse, or OUTCOME_WAIT_TIMEOUT elapse detected by that evaluation within the same evaluation. **[ASSUMPTION]** — the interval value is configuration pending calibration.
14. IF a transition into DECISION_PENDING is requested for a Recovery_Case whose decision-cycle counter equals MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL reject that transition and SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to STOPPED with the transition reason DECISION_CYCLE_LIMIT_REACHED.
15. IF the Outcome_Monitor cannot obtain authoritative Payment_Provider payment state for a Recovery_Case within the configured outcome query retry limit, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to FAILED where the Recovery_Window end timestamp has been reached, SHALL otherwise leave the Recovery_Case in WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME for re-evaluation at the next LIFECYCLE_EVALUATION_INTERVAL without incrementing any counter, and SHALL write an OUTCOME_QUERY_FAILED Audit_Record indicating that authoritative payment state was unavailable. **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** on Payment_Provider payment-state query availability and failure modes.

### Requirement 3: Risk Cause Diagnosis

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want Revora to determine why a payment is at risk using structured payment data first, so that diagnosis is cheap, repeatable, and only uses AI where genuine ambiguity exists.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Recovery_Case enters DETECTED, THE Diagnosis_Engine SHALL classify the Risk_Cause by applying the declared mapping table from Payment_Provider failure code and structured Payment_Event fields to the Risk_Cause enumeration, reading only fields already persisted by Event_Ingestion and issuing no request to the Payment_Provider. **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** on provider failure code values and field names; the mapping table contents are populated during design-phase verification.
2. WHEN the declared mapping table yields exactly one Risk_Cause for a Recovery_Case, THE Diagnosis_Engine SHALL record that Risk_Cause, the confidence value 1.0, the matched mapping rule identifier as evidence, and the method value DETERMINISTIC, and SHALL not invoke the Reasoning_Layer for that Recovery_Case decision cycle.
3. IF the declared mapping table yields no Risk_Cause, yields two or more conflicting Risk_Causes, or matches only a rule marked as requiring interpretation of unstructured context, THEN THE Diagnosis_Engine SHALL request exactly one Risk_Cause hypothesis from the Reasoning_Layer, SHALL include in that request only persisted payment failure fields and Recovery_Case metadata while excluding customer contact identifiers, and SHALL wait no longer than REASONING_TIMEOUT for a response.
4. THE Diagnosis_Engine SHALL emit exactly one active Diagnosis record per Recovery_Case decision cycle containing the fields cause, confidence, evidence, and method, where cause is a member of the Risk_Cause enumeration INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS, EXPIRED_PAYMENT_METHOD, BANK_OR_NETWORK_FAILURE, TECHNICAL_ISSUE, ABANDONMENT, CUSTOMER_ACTION_REQUIRED, FRAUD_OR_RISK_SIGNAL, UNKNOWN; confidence is a value between 0.0 and 1.0 inclusive; evidence identifies either the matched mapping rule identifier or the Reasoning_Layer response that produced the cause; and method is one of DETERMINISTIC, AI_ASSISTED, REJECTED_AI_OUTPUT, FALLBACK_UNKNOWN.
5. ### Requirement 7: Incremental Value Optimization and Action Selection

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want Revora to select the action with the highest positive net incremental value and to choose no action when nothing is economically justified, so that I stop paying for interventions that do not change outcomes.

Worked reference from the project brief, payment_amount ₹20,000: DO_NOTHING 20% baseline, ₹0 incremental; RETRY 35%, +15%, ₹3,000 expected incremental; PAYMENT_LINK 60%, +40%, ₹8,000 expected incremental; a voice channel at 62%, +42%, ₹8,400 expected incremental carries higher execution cost and may lose on net value. **[FACT]** as an illustrative arithmetic example; the probability figures themselves are **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** as real-world estimates.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Intervention_Simulator has recorded intervention_recovery_probability, action_cost, risk_cost, and customer_cost for every Candidate_Action in the Candidate_Action set of a Recovery_Case, THE Value_Optimizer SHALL compute for every Candidate_Action in that set incremental_probability as intervention_recovery_probability minus baseline_recovery_probability, retaining a negative figure where intervention_recovery_probability falls below baseline_recovery_probability.
2. WHEN the Value_Optimizer has computed incremental_probability for a Candidate_Action, THE Value_Optimizer SHALL compute expected_incremental_revenue as payment_amount multiplied by incremental_probability, producing a negative figure where incremental_probability is negative.
3. WHEN the Value_Optimizer has computed expected_incremental_revenue for a Candidate_Action, THE Value_Optimizer SHALL compute net_recovery_value as expected_incremental_revenue minus action_cost minus risk_cost minus customer_cost.
4. WHEN the Value_Optimizer has computed net_recovery_value for every Candidate_Action in the Candidate_Action set and has applied every exclusion rule, THE Value_Optimizer SHALL select the Candidate_Action holding the greatest net_recovery_value among the Candidate_Actions that carry no exclusion reason, that are not marked UNAVAILABLE, whose net_recovery_value is greater than or equal to MIN_NET_VALUE_THRESHOLD, and whose incremental_probability is greater than or equal to MIN_INCREMENTAL_PROBABILITY, resolving equal net_recovery_value figures in favour of the Candidate_Action holding the lower sum of action_cost, risk_cost, and customer_cost, and resolving a remaining tie by the declared Candidate_Action precedence order.
5. IF no Candidate_Action satisfies both MIN_NET_VALUE_THRESHOLD and MIN_INCREMENTAL_PROBABILITY after every exclusion rule is applied, THEN THE Value_Optimizer SHALL select whichever of DO_NOTHING and WAIT holds the greater net_recovery_value, SHALL select DO_NOTHING where the two net_recovery_value figures are equal, and SHALL record the selection reason NO_POSITIVE_VALUE.
6. WHERE baseline_recovery_probability is greater than or equal to HIGH_BASELINE_THRESHOLD, IF no Candidate_Action carrying no exclusion reason satisfies both MIN_NET_VALUE_THRESHOLD and MIN_INCREMENTAL_PROBABILITY, THEN THE Value_Optimizer SHALL select whichever of DO_NOTHING and WAIT holds the greater net_recovery_value, SHALL select DO_NOTHING where the two net_recovery_value figures are equal, and SHALL record the selection reason HIGH_BASELINE_NO_INTERVENTION.
7. IF the sum of action_cost, risk_cost, and customer_cost for a Candidate_Action whose expected_incremental_revenue is greater than zero, divided by that expected_incremental_revenue, exceeds MAX_COST_TO_VALUE_RATIO, THEN THE Value_Optimizer SHALL exclude that Candidate_Action from selection and SHALL record the exclusion reason COST_RATIO_EXCEEDED.
8. WHERE the Candidate_Action holding the greatest intervention_recovery_probability among Candidate_Actions carrying no exclusion reason differs from the selected Candidate_Action, THE Value_Optimizer SHALL record both Candidate_Actions in the Recommendation together with their expected_incremental_revenue, their summed cost terms, and their net_recovery_value figures, and SHALL record the divergence reason HIGHER_PROBABILITY_LOWER_NET_VALUE.
9. THE Value_Optimizer SHALL record in every Recommendation the selected Candidate_Action, every rejected Candidate_Action, the selection reason, every exclusion reason, and per Candidate_Action the figures incremental_probability, expected_incremental_revenue, action_cost, risk_cost, customer_cost, and net_recovery_value.
10. THE Value_Optimizer SHALL rank Candidate_Actions by net_recovery_value alone, taking no ranking input from intervention_recovery_probability magnitude, from Reasoning_Layer free text, or from any other field the Reasoning_Layer produced.
11. WHERE the Recommendation includes Reasoning_Layer explanation text, THE Value_Optimizer SHALL store that text in a field marked explanation-only, SHALL keep the selected Candidate_Action derived from the numeric comparison, and SHALL record that the text held no influence on the selection.
12. THE Value_Optimizer SHALL compute and store every currency figure as an integer count of minor currency units, SHALL apply half-up rounding at each step that produces a currency figure, SHALL hold probability values at 4 decimal places, and SHALL compute every reported aggregate figure as the sum of the stored per-Recovery_Case integer figures so that the reported aggregate equals that sum exactly.
13. IF a Candidate_Action holds a recorded baseline_recovery_probability or intervention_recovery_probability outside 0.0 to 1.0 inclusive, or holds a missing action_cost, risk_cost, or customer_cost, THEN THE Value_Optimizer SHALL exclude that Candidate_Action from selection and SHALL record the exclusion reason INVALID_ESTIMATE_INPUT.
14. IF the expected_incremental_revenue of a Candidate_Action is less than or equal to zero, THEN THE Value_Optimizer SHALL exclude that Candidate_Action from selection, SHALL record the exclusion reason NON_POSITIVE_INCREMENTAL_VALUE, and SHALL perform no MAX_COST_TO_VALUE_RATIO division for that Candidate_Action.
15. WHEN the Value_Optimizer evaluates a Candidate_Action set whose recorded numeric estimates and payment_amount match those of an earlier evaluated Candidate_Action set, THE Value_Optimizer SHALL select the same Candidate_Action and SHALL report the same per Candidate_Action value figures, whether or not Reasoning_Layer explanation text is present.

### Requirement 8: Policy Engine as Final Authority

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Value_Optimizer produces a Recommendation, THE Policy_Engine SHALL evaluate the Recommendation against the declared ordered check sequence and SHALL issue exactly one Policy_Decision drawn from APPROVED, BLOCKED, DEFERRED, or ESCALATE, carrying exactly one primary reason taken from the lowest-ordered non-pass check, before any execution of that Recommendation occurs.

4. IF the customer of a Recovery_Case holds opt-out status, or a consent record required for a customer-visible Candidate_Action is absent or expired at the evaluation timestamp, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue BLOCKED for every customer-visible Candidate_Action carrying the reason CUSTOMER_OPTED_OUT or CONSENT_MISSING respectively, and THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to STOPPED where opt-out status holds.
5. IF the persisted state of a Recovery_Case carries a Payment_Provider fraud or risk flag, or the recorded Risk_Cause of that Recovery_Case is FRAUD_OR_RISK_SIGNAL, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue ESCALATE with the reason FRAUD_OR_RISK_FLAG, SHALL block every Candidate_Action other than DO_NOTHING and WAIT from automated execution, and THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to ESCALATED.
6. IF the executed-action counter of a Recovery_Case equals or exceeds MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS at the evaluation timestamp, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue BLOCKED with the reason MAX_ATTEMPTS_REACHED for every Candidate_Action other than DO_NOTHING and WAIT, and THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to STOPPED or ESCALATED according to the configured escalation rule.
7. IF the customer-message counter of a Recovery_Case equals or exceeds MAX_CUSTOMER_MESSAGES at the evaluation timestamp, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue BLOCKED with the reason MAX_MESSAGES_REACHED for every customer-visible Candidate_Action, and SHALL leave Candidate_Actions that are not customer-visible eligible for the remaining checks.
8. IF the interval between the evaluation timestamp and the most recent outbound action timestamp of a Recovery_Case is shorter than COOLDOWN_INTERVAL, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue DEFERRED with the reason COOLDOWN_ACTIVE together with an earliest permitted execution timestamp equal to that most recent outbound action timestamp plus COOLDOWN_INTERVAL, and SHALL issue BLOCKED with the reason WINDOW_EXPIRED in place of DEFERRED where that earliest permitted execution timestamp falls after the end of the Recovery_Window.
9. IF the Recovery_Window of a Recovery_Case has elapsed at the evaluation timestamp, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue BLOCKED with the reason WINDOW_EXPIRED for every Candidate_Action and THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to EXPIRED.
10. IF a persisted execution-intent record carrying the same Idempotency_Key as the Recommendation exists in the state ATTEMPTED, CONFIRMED, or UNCERTAIN, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue BLOCKED with the reason DUPLICATE_ACTION and SHALL return the recorded execution-intent state with the decision.
11. IF a human owner is assigned to a Recovery_Case, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue BLOCKED for every automated Candidate_Action with the reason HUMAN_OWNERSHIP, and SHALL leave actions initiated by the assigned human owner outside the scope of that block.
12. WHEN the Policy_Engine issues any Policy_Decision, THE Policy_Engine SHALL write exactly one Audit_Record, before any execution authorization is released, containing the issued Policy_Decision, the primary reason, the outcome of each of the twelve checks in declared evaluation order, the rule set version identifier, the evaluation timestamp, and the Recovery_Case state observed at that evaluation timestamp.
13. WHERE the selected Candidate_Action of a Recommendation is DO_NOTHING or WAIT, THE Policy_Engine SHALL record a Policy_Decision and its Audit_Record and SHALL issue no Payment_Provider request and no Communication_Provider request.
14. THE Policy_Engine SHALL derive every Policy_Decision solely from the Recommendation, the persisted Recovery_Case state, the declared rule set of the recorded rule set version, and the evaluation timestamp, and SHALL produce an identical Policy_Decision, an identical primary reason, and identical ordered check outcomes on every repeated evaluation holding those four inputs identical.
15. WHEN every check in the declared evaluation order returns pass for the selected Candidate_Action of a Recommendation, THE Policy_Engine SHALL issue APPROVED carrying the Recovery_Case identifier, the selected Candidate_Action, the Idempotency_Key, the rule set version identifier, and an approval expiry timestamp, and SHALL authorize at most one execution attempt per issued APPROVED Policy_Decision.
16. IF the Policy_Engine issues BLOCKED, DEFERRED, or ESCALATE for a Recommendation, THEN THE Revora system SHALL issue no Payment_Provider request and no Communication_Provider request for that Recommendation, and THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL leave the executed-action counter, the customer-message counter, and the most recent outbound action timestamp of the Recovery_Case unchanged.
17. IF an input required by any check in the declared evaluation order is unavailable at the evaluation timestamp, THEN THE Policy_Engine SHALL record that check outcome as UNAVAILABLE, SHALL issue BLOCKED with the reason POLICY_INPUT_UNAVAILABLE, and SHALL issue no APPROVED Policy_Decision for that Recommendation.

### Requirement 9: Bounded, Idempotent Execution of an Approved Recovery Action

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want approved actions executed exactly once with confirmed results, so that a customer is never charged twice, messaged twice, or told a link exists when the provider call failed.

MVP execution scope is one real Payment_Provider recovery action. Payment link creation is the intended candidate, conditional on design-phase verification of provider capability. **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** on the exact provider API and its constraints; the requirement is written against a generic capability so that a verified alternative can substitute without rewriting the specification.

Exactly-once external effect rests on the durably committed execution-intent record plus reconciliation against authoritative provider state; whether the Payment_Provider or the Communication_Provider exposes a provider-side idempotency mechanism is **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** and must be verified before design closes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Execution_Engine receives an execution request, THE Execution_Engine SHALL read the Recovery_Case current state, executed-action counter, customer-message counter, most-recent-action timestamp, and verified payment state from the authoritative persistent store, discarding any corresponding values carried in the request, before performing any external call.
2. WHEN the Execution_Engine holds the reloaded Recovery_Case state, THE Execution_Engine SHALL re-request Policy_Engine evaluation against that reloaded state, SHALL proceed only where the re-issued Policy_Decision is APPROVED, and SHALL abandon the execution attempt with no external call and an EXECUTION_ABANDONED_POLICY Audit_Record for every other Policy_Decision value.

THE Execution_Engine SHALL derive the Idempotency_Key deterministically from the Recovery_Case identifier, the Candidate_Action type, and the attempt ordinal, SHALL produce the identical Idempotency_Key for every re-execution of that same attempt ordinal, and SHALL advance the attempt ordinal only where a further Policy_Decision of APPROVED authorizes an additional action on that Recovery_Case.
4. WHEN the Execution_Engine begins an external call, THE Execution_Engine SHALL persist an execution-intent record carrying the Idempotency_Key, the Recovery_Case identifier, the Candidate_Action type, the attempt start timestamp, and the state ATTEMPTED, and SHALL issue the external call only after that record is durably committed.
5. IF an execution-intent record already exists for a derived Idempotency_Key, THEN THE Execution_Engine SHALL issue no additional external call, SHALL return the recorded result where the recorded state is CONFIRMED or FAILED, and SHALL return an unresolved result requiring reconciliation where the recorded state is ATTEMPTED or UNCERTAIN.
6. WHEN an external call returns a success confirmation, THE Execution_Engine SHALL persist the provider-supplied response identifier together with the state CONFIRMED on the execution-intent record for that Idempotency_Key, and SHALL treat the action as successful only after that record is durably committed.
7. THE Execution_Engine SHALL report an action as successful, present it as successful on the Merchant_Dashboard, and permit any customer-visible statement that the action exists, only while the execution-intent record for that action holds CONFIRMED with a persisted Payment_Provider or Communication_Provider response identifier.
8. IF an external call returns an error response, THEN THE Execution_Engine SHALL set the execution-intent state to FAILED, SHALL record the provider-reported failure detail in a failure Audit_Record, SHALL issue no further external call within that execution attempt, and SHALL leave the executed-action counter and the customer-message counter unchanged.
9. IF an external call produces no response within PROVIDER_CALL_TIMEOUT, or returns a response the Execution_Engine cannot classify as either success or error, THEN THE Execution_Engine SHALL set the execution-intent state to UNCERTAIN, SHALL report no result as successful, and SHALL issue no further external call for that Recovery_Case until reconciliation resolves that execution-intent record. **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** on provider response semantics.
10. WHEN an execution-intent record reaches CONFIRMED, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL increment the executed-action counter exactly once for that Idempotency_Key, set the most-recent-action timestamp to the confirmation timestamp, and transition the Recovery_Case to WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME.
11. WHEN an execution-intent record for a customer-visible Candidate_Action reaches CONFIRMED, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL increment the customer-message counter exactly once for that Idempotency_Key.
12. WHILE an execution attempt for a Recovery_Case is in progress, THE Execution_Engine SHALL hold an exclusive lock keyed by the Recovery_Case identifier, granted to at most one worker at any instant, carrying a lease no longer than EXECUTION_LOCK_LEASE, and released on the execution-intent record reaching CONFIRMED, FAILED, or UNCERTAIN or on lease expiry, whichever occurs first.
13. IF the exclusive lock for a Recovery_Case is unavailable, THEN THE Execution_Engine SHALL abandon the execution attempt without issuing any external call, SHALL leave the Recovery_Case state and counters unchanged, and SHALL write a CONCURRENT_EXECUTION_PREVENTED Audit_Record.
14. IF a call to the Communication_Provider cannot be established, or the Communication_Provider reports itself unavailable before accepting the request, THEN THE Execution_Engine SHALL set the execution-intent state to FAILED and THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL return the Recovery_Case to DECISION_PENDING where the remaining Recovery_Window, MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS, and MAX_CUSTOMER_MESSAGES bounds still permit a further action, and to STOPPED otherwise.
15. WHILE an execution-intent record holds UNCERTAIN, or holds ATTEMPTED for longer than PROVIDER_CALL_TIMEOUT, THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL reconcile that record against authoritative provider state at intervals no longer than EXECUTION_RECONCILIATION_INTERVAL, and SHALL set the record to CONFIRMED with the provider-supplied response identifier where authoritative provider state shows the external effect exists, or to FAILED where authoritative provider state shows no such effect exists.
16. WHEN the Execution_Engine starts, THE Execution_Engine SHALL set every execution-intent record still holding ATTEMPTED whose attempt start timestamp precedes the start time by more than PROVIDER_CALL_TIMEOUT to UNCERTAIN, write an EXECUTION_RESULT_UNKNOWN Audit_Record, and route that record to reconciliation instead of repeating the external call.
17. IF reconciliation does not resolve an execution-intent record to CONFIRMED or FAILED within MAX_EXECUTION_RECONCILIATION_ATTEMPTS, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to ESCALATED with the reason EXECUTION_RESULT_UNVERIFIABLE and THE Execution_Engine SHALL issue no further external call for that Recovery_Case.

### Requirement 10: Outcome Observation and Verification

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want recovery declared only against verified payment state, so that reported recoveries match money that actually arrived.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL treat Payment_Provider verified payment state, obtained from an authoritative provider state read for the provider payment identifier of a Recovery_Case, as the authoritative source of payment truth for every Recovery_Case, and SHALL declare no Recovery_Case RECOVERED on the basis of an inbound event alone.
2. WHEN the Outcome_Monitor receives an event indicating payment success for a Recovery_Case, THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL perform an authoritative provider state read within OUTCOME_READ_LATENCY_BOUND of that event being persisted and SHALL declare RECOVERED only where that read reports a paid or captured state for the provider payment identifier of that Recovery_Case. **[ASSUMPTION]** on the latency bound.
3. WHEN an authoritative provider state read confirms a Recovery_Case payment as paid, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to RECOVERED and record the recovered amount as the paid amount reported by that read in minor currency units, the provider-reported payment timestamp as the recovery timestamp, and the elapsed seconds between the detection timestamp and that recovery timestamp.
4. WHEN an authoritative provider state read confirms a Recovery_Case payment as paid while an action for that Recovery_Case is scheduled or queued and no execution-intent record exists for that action, THE Execution_Engine SHALL cancel that action before any external call is issued, SHALL leave the executed-action counter unchanged, and SHALL write an ACTION_CANCELLED_PAYMENT_RECEIVED Audit_Record.
5. IF an execution-intent record for a Recovery_Case action already holds the state ATTEMPTED, CONFIRMED, or UNCERTAIN at the moment an authoritative provider state read confirms that Recovery_Case payment as paid, THEN THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL record that action as POST_PAYMENT_ACTION and THE Metrics_Engine SHALL count that action exactly once in unnecessary_action_count.
6. WHILE two or more payment-state signals for the provider payment identifier of a Recovery_Case report different payment states, THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL hold the Recovery_Case in WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME, SHALL declare no recovery for that Recovery_Case, and SHALL schedule authoritative provider state reads at intervals of PAYMENT_STATE_RECONCILIATION_INTERVAL up to a maximum of MAX_PAYMENT_STATE_READ_ATTEMPTS. **[ASSUMPTION]** on the interval and the attempt bound.
7. IF authoritative provider state for a Recovery_Case cannot be read after MAX_PAYMENT_STATE_READ_ATTEMPTS consecutive attempts, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL transition the Recovery_Case to ESCALATED with the reason PAYMENT_STATE_UNVERIFIABLE, SHALL preserve the last recorded payment state and the unresolved payment_amount, and SHALL declare no recovery for that Recovery_Case.
8. WHEN a Recovery_Case reaches RECOVERED with zero executed Revora actions, THE Metrics_Engine SHALL classify the outcome as Natural_Recovery.
9. WHEN a Recovery_Case reaches RECOVERED with one or more executed Revora actions, THE Metrics_Engine SHALL classify the outcome as Observed_Recovery.
10. WHEN a Recovery_Case reaches a Terminal_State other than RECOVERED, THE Metrics_Engine SHALL record the payment_amount exactly once in unresolved_revenue together with the Terminal_State and its recorded reason, and SHALL exclude that payment_amount from observed_recovered_revenue and natural_recovered_revenue.
11. WHEN the Outcome_Monitor receives a payment-success event for a Recovery_Case already holding RECOVERED whose provider payment identifier matches the recorded recovery, THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL discard that event without altering the recorded recovered amount, recovery timestamp, or outcome classification, SHALL issue no additional authoritative provider state read, SHALL write a DUPLICATE_RECOVERY_EVENT_DISCARDED Audit_Record, and THE Metrics_Engine SHALL count the recovered amount of that Recovery_Case exactly once regardless of the number of payment-success events received.
12. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL count as an executed Revora action of a Recovery_Case exactly those actions holding an execution-intent record in the state CONFIRMED and not recorded as POST_PAYMENT_ACTION, and SHALL withhold Natural_Recovery and Observed_Recovery classification for that Recovery_Case while any execution-intent record for that Recovery_Case holds the state UNCERTAIN.
13. IF an authoritative provider state read for a Recovery_Case reports a payment state other than paid or captured after an event indicating payment success was received for that Recovery_Case, THEN THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL withhold the RECOVERED transition, SHALL hold the Recovery_Case in WAITING_FOR_OUTCOME, and SHALL write a PAYMENT_STATE_CONFLICT Audit_Record naming the conflicting payment-state signals.
14. WHEN the Outcome_Monitor receives an event indicating payment success for a Recovery_Case already holding a Terminal_State other than RECOVERED, THE Outcome_Monitor SHALL perform an authoritative provider state read, and where that read confirms the payment as paid, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL reconcile the Recovery_Case to RECOVERED, SHALL write a DELAYED_RECOVERY_RECONCILED Audit_Record naming the superseded Terminal_State, and THE Metrics_Engine SHALL restate unresolved_revenue and recovered amount for the reporting period containing the recovery timestamp so that the recovered amount is counted exactly once.

### Requirement 12: Recovery Metrics and Outcome Class Separation

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want metrics that separate money that arrived on its own from money that arrived after intervention from money proven incremental, so that I am not sold activity as outcome.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute revenue_at_risk for a reporting period as the sum of payment_amount, expressed in minor currency units, across the reporting period cohort, where the reporting period cohort is the set of Recovery_Cases whose detection timestamp falls within the half-open interval beginning at the recorded reporting period start timestamp and ending at the recorded reporting period end timestamp.
2. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute observed_recovered_revenue as the sum of confirmed recovered amounts, counted once per Recovery_Case, across reporting period cohort Recovery_Cases classified Observed_Recovery as of the recorded computation timestamp.
3. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute natural_recovered_revenue as the sum of confirmed recovered amounts, counted once per Recovery_Case, across reporting period cohort Recovery_Cases classified Natural_Recovery as of the recorded computation timestamp.
4. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute incremental_recovered_revenue only from the Control_Group versus Treatment_Group comparison produced by the Experiment_Engine for a completed experiment whose reported incremental lift uncertainty interval excludes zero, that is, only from recoveries classified Attributed_Recovery, and SHALL present that figure together with the experiment identifier, the Recovery_Case count per group, and the reported uncertainty interval.
5. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute for the reporting period cohort recovery_rate as the count of Recovery_Cases whose Terminal_State is RECOVERED divided by the cohort Recovery_Case count, intervention_rate as the count of Recovery_Cases carrying at least one CONFIRMED executed action divided by the cohort Recovery_Case count, action_success_rate as the count of CONFIRMED executed actions followed by a verified recovery on the same Recovery_Case within OUTCOME_WAIT_TIMEOUT divided by the count of CONFIRMED executed actions, escalation_rate as the count of Recovery_Cases whose Terminal_State is ESCALATED divided by the cohort Recovery_Case count, average_time_to_recovery as the mean elapsed hours from detection timestamp to verified recovery timestamp across Recovery_Cases whose Terminal_State is RECOVERED, blocked_case_count as the count of Recovery_Cases whose Terminal_State is BLOCKED, unnecessary_action_count as the count of actions recorded POST_PAYMENT_ACTION, and unresolved_revenue as the sum of payment_amount across cohort Recovery_Cases whose Terminal_State is other than RECOVERED, reporting the value UNDEFINED for any listed rate whose denominator equals zero.
6. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute total_recovery_cost as the sum of realized action_cost, expressed in minor currency units, across CONFIRMED executed actions on reporting period cohort Recovery_Cases, realized_recovery_cost per Recovery_Case as the sum of realized action_cost across CONFIRMED executed actions on that Recovery_Case, and net_recovered_revenue as observed_recovered_revenue minus total_recovery_cost.
7. WHEN the Metrics_Engine reports recovery_rate for a reporting period, THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report in the same view for that same reporting period the count of Recovery_Cases whose Terminal_State is RECOVERED, observed_recovered_revenue, total_recovery_cost, and net_recovered_revenue.
8. IF recovery_rate increases between two consecutive reporting periods of equal duration while net_recovered_revenue decreases across those same two periods, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL raise a COST_OUTPACING_RECOVERY finding, presented in the same view as the reported recovery_rate, stating the recovery_rate change, the net_recovered_revenue change, and naming every segment whose total_recovery_cost increase exceeds its observed_recovered_revenue increase.
9. IF observed_recovered_revenue for a reporting period is greater than zero while the Experiment_Engine reports for that reporting period an incremental lift whose uncertainty interval contains zero, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL label the reported observed_recovered_revenue CAUSALITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED in every presentation surface of that figure.
10. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report every metric named in criteria 1 through 6 segmented by Risk_Cause, payment_amount band drawn from the configured non-overlapping and exhaustive band set, selected Candidate_Action, and Policy_Decision outcome, presenting the aggregate value together with the segment values.
11. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL label every metric computed wholly or partly from a Synthetic_Dataset with the provenance value SYNTHETIC in every presentation surface and every export of that metric.
12. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL derive every reported metric from persisted Recovery_Case records, Audit_Records, and verified payment state, recording with every reported figure the reporting period start timestamp, the reporting period end timestamp, and the computation timestamp.
13. IF no completed experiment whose reported incremental lift uncertainty interval excludes zero exists for a reporting period, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report incremental_recovered_revenue for that reporting period as NOT_ESTABLISHED carrying no numeric value, and SHALL present neither observed_recovered_revenue nor natural_recovered_revenue as incremental_recovered_revenue.
14. IF the realized_recovery_cost of a Recovery_Case whose Terminal_State is RECOVERED divided by the confirmed recovered amount of that Recovery_Case exceeds MAX_COST_TO_VALUE_RATIO, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL raise a RECOVERY_COST_EXCEEDS_VALUE finding, presented in the same view as the reported observed_recovered_revenue for the reporting period of that Recovery_Case, naming that Recovery_Case, its confirmed recovered amount, its realized_recovery_cost, and the selected Candidate_Action. Applying MAX_COST_TO_VALUE_RATIO to realized rather than expected figures is an **[ASSUMPTION]** to be confirmed in design.
15. IF the count of Recovery_Cases whose Terminal_State is RECOVERED decreases between two consecutive reporting periods of equal duration while observed_recovered_revenue increases across those same two periods, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL raise a RECOVERY_MIX_SHIFT finding, presented in the same view as the reported recovery_rate, stating the recovered Recovery_Case count change, the observed_recovered_revenue change, and naming the payment_amount bands contributing the increase.
16. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL record per Recovery_Case the decision-cycle count consumed by that Recovery_Case and SHALL report, for the reporting period cohort, the count of Recovery_Cases that consumed one or more decision cycles while executing zero CONFIRMED actions.

### Requirement 13: Control Versus Treatment Evaluation and Causality Discipline

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want a controlled comparison against my baseline workflow, so that I can tell whether Revora created recovered revenue rather than merely being present when payments arrived.

The reference experiment shape from the project brief is 500 Control_Group cases on the Baseline_Workflow against 500 Treatment_Group cases on Revora decisioning. Whether 500 per arm detects the true effect is **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]**; criterion 4 makes the required sample size a computed output rather than an assumed constant.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Recovery_Case is created while an experiment holds the state ACTIVE and that Recovery_Case satisfies the eligibility criteria recorded for that experiment, THE Experiment_Engine SHALL assign the Recovery_Case to Control_Group or Treatment_Group using a deterministic randomization function whose inputs are limited to the Recovery_Case identifier, the experiment identifier, and EXPERIMENT_ALLOCATION_RATIO **[ASSUMPTION]**, such that repeated evaluation of the same inputs yields the same group for the lifetime of the experiment.
2. THE Experiment_Engine SHALL persist the group assignment of a Recovery_Case before any Diagnosis or Recommendation for that Recovery_Case is produced, and SHALL reject every subsequent request to change a persisted group assignment.
3. WHILE a Recovery_Case is assigned to Control_Group, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL apply the Baseline_Workflow, SHALL suppress every Candidate_Action selected by Revora decisioning so that no execution intent derived from a Recommendation reaches the Execution_Engine, and SHALL record the suppressed Recommendation for comparison without executing it.
4. WHEN a Merchant defines an experiment, THE Experiment_Engine SHALL compute and record, before Recovery_Case assignment begins, the primary metric, every secondary metric, the eligibility criteria, the allocation ratio, the assumed Control_Group baseline rate, the minimum detectable effect, EXPERIMENT_SIGNIFICANCE_LEVEL **[ASSUMPTION]**, EXPERIMENT_POWER **[ASSUMPTION]**, the analysis method, and the required sample size per group computed as the smallest per-group Recovery_Case count that detects the recorded minimum detectable effect at the recorded significance level and power. **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** on real-world calibration of these inputs.
5. THE Experiment_Engine SHALL freeze and record the version identifiers of the Baseline_Workflow definition, the policy rule set, the Baseline_Model, and the Intervention_Simulator from experiment activation until that experiment reaches a terminal experiment state.
6. WHEN an experiment has reached its recorded required sample size per group and every assigned Recovery_Case has reached a Terminal_State or has exhausted RECOVERY_WINDOW_DURATION plus OUTCOME_WAIT_TIMEOUT, THE Experiment_Engine SHALL report recovery rate, recovered revenue, average time to recovery, intervention rate, cost per recovery, and incremental lift for each group, computed over the same assignment interval for both groups.
7. THE Experiment_Engine SHALL report incremental lift only together with its uncertainty interval at EXPERIMENT_CONFIDENCE_LEVEL **[ASSUMPTION]**, the recorded analysis method, and the count of Recovery_Cases in each group, and SHALL label any reported lift whose uncertainty interval contains zero as CAUSALITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED.
8. THE Experiment_Engine SHALL classify a recovery as Attributed_Recovery only where the recovery belongs to an experiment whose state is COMPLETED, whose per-group Recovery_Case count is at least the recorded required sample size per group, whose reported incremental lift uncertainty interval on the recorded primary metric excludes zero and lies entirely above zero, and which carries none of the labels UNDERPOWERED, INVALIDATED, or SYNTHETIC.
9. IF an experiment reports fewer Recovery_Cases per group than the recorded required sample size per group, including any result requested before the experiment reaches the state COMPLETED, THEN THE Experiment_Engine SHALL label the result UNDERPOWERED and SHALL withhold Attributed_Recovery classification from every Recovery_Case in that experiment.
10. THE Experiment_Engine SHALL report Control_Group and Treatment_Group results for every recorded segment together with the aggregate result, and SHALL label every segment result whose per-group Recovery_Case count is below the recorded required sample size per group UNDERPOWERED.
11. THE Experiment_Engine SHALL report results for the primary metric recorded at experiment definition time and for every secondary metric recorded at experiment definition time, and SHALL label every secondary metric result EXPLORATORY and exclude it from Attributed_Recovery classification.
12. WHERE experiment inputs originate from a Synthetic_Dataset, THE Experiment_Engine SHALL label the experiment result SYNTHETIC, SHALL record the generation assumptions, the embedded ground truth lift, and the generation seed that reproduces the identical Synthetic_Dataset on re-generation, and SHALL report the difference between the measured incremental lift and the embedded ground truth lift.
13. THE Experiment_Engine SHALL present the question "does Revora decisioning recover more revenue than the Baseline_Workflow within policy limits and without added customer or operational cost" as a reported per-group comparison of net_recovered_revenue, intervention_rate, customer-message count per Recovery_Case, and blocked_case_count, each reported with the direction and size of the Treatment_Group minus Control_Group difference.
14. IF the group assignment for an eligible Recovery_Case cannot be persisted before Diagnosis for that Recovery_Case begins, THEN THE Experiment_Engine SHALL exclude that Recovery_Case from both Control_Group and Treatment_Group and THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL apply the Baseline_Workflow to that Recovery_Case.
15. IF an executed action on a Control_Group Recovery_Case is not an action defined by the frozen Baseline_Workflow definition, THEN THE Experiment_Engine SHALL label that Recovery_Case CONTAMINATED, SHALL exclude that Recovery_Case from every reported group result, and SHALL report the contaminated Recovery_Case count and the excluded Recovery_Case count with every experiment result.
16. IF the frozen Baseline_Workflow definition, policy rule set version, Baseline_Model version, or Intervention_Simulator version changes while an experiment holds the state ACTIVE, THEN THE Experiment_Engine SHALL label that experiment INVALIDATED, SHALL stop further Recovery_Case assignment to that experiment, and SHALL withhold Attributed_Recovery classification from every Recovery_Case in that experiment.

### Requirement 15: Recovery Memory and Bounded Learning

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want Revora to learn from completed cases through explicit, versioned model promotion, so that estimates improve over time while policy authority stays under human control.

Recovery_Memory observations record decisions that humans and prior model versions already made, so a model trained on them reproduces those past choices rather than measuring outcomes **[INFERENCE]**; criteria 4 and 12 keep the decision provenance of every observation inspectable so that skew toward historically chosen Candidate_Actions and away from never-chosen ones is detectable before a promotion. 1. WHEN the Recovery_Case_Manager records a Recovery_Case transition to a Terminal_State, THE Recovery_Memory SHALL persist exactly one observation for that Recovery_Case within the same atomic transaction, holding the feature values, the Diagnosis fields cause, confidence, and method, the selected Candidate_Action, the Policy_Decision, the outcome classification, the realized action_cost, and the experiment group assignment.
2. THE Recovery_Memory SHALL record for every stored observation the data provenance value REAL where the observation derives from a Payment_Event received from the Payment_Provider, and the data provenance value SYNTHETIC where the observation derives from a Synthetic_Dataset.
3. THE Baseline_Model and the Intervention_Simulator SHALL label a produced estimate with the data provenance value REAL only where every Recovery_Memory observation behind that estimate carries the provenance value REAL, and SHALL label the estimate SYNTHETIC where one or more observations behind that estimate carry the provenance value SYNTHETIC.
4. THE Recovery_Memory SHALL store for every observation the experiment group assignment, the executed-action count, the customer-message count, the decision-cycle count, and the decision source value AUTOMATED, HUMAN_OVERRIDE, or BASELINE_WORKFLOW together with the acting Merchant_User identifier where the decision source is HUMAN_OVERRIDE, and SHALL make the observations holding an executed-action count of zero selectable as a distinct Baseline_Model training set.
5. THE Revora system SHALL activate a Baseline_Model version or an Intervention_Simulator version only through an explicit version promotion record holding the promoted version identifier, the prior version identifier, the promotion timestamp, the approving Merchant_User identifier, the training observation count, and the count of training observations carrying the provenance value SYNTHETIC.
6. THE Revora system SHALL change the Policy_Engine rule set only through a recorded configuration change carrying a new rule set version identifier, the change timestamp, and the approving Merchant_User identifier, and THE Policy_Engine SHALL derive no policy check and no policy threshold from Recovery_Memory contents.
7. WHILE an experiment is active, THE Revora system SHALL apply to every Recovery_Case assigned to that experiment the Baseline_Model version and the Intervention_Simulator version frozen at experiment definition time, regardless of any version promotion recorded while that experiment is active.
8. WHEN a model version promotion is recorded, THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report metrics for Recovery_Cases decided under the promoted version separately from metrics for Recovery_Cases decided under each prior version, segmenting by the model version identifiers recorded on each Recovery_Case.
9. THE Recovery_Memory SHALL retain for every stored estimate the producing model version identifier, the estimation method, and the identifier of the promotion record that activated that version.
10. IF a version promotion request omits the promoted version identifier, the prior version identifier, the promotion timestamp, or the approving Merchant_User identifier, THEN THE Revora system SHALL reject the activation, continue producing estimates from the currently active version, and write a MODEL_PROMOTION_REJECTED Audit_Record identifying the omitted fields.
11. WHEN a Baseline_Model training run or an Intervention_Simulator training run completes, THE Revora system SHALL record the produced version with the state INACTIVE and SHALL continue producing estimates from the currently active promoted version until a version promotion is recorded.
12. WHEN a Merchant_User requests the training-set composition of a candidate model version, THE Recovery_Memory SHALL report the observation count per decision source value, per Risk_Cause, per selected Candidate_Action, per Policy_Decision, and per provenance value, together with every Candidate_Action holding zero observations in that training set.

### Requirement 16: Failure Tolerance, Event Canonicalization, and Data Consistency

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want Revora to fail safely rather than act blindly, so that infrastructure faults produce delayed decisions instead of duplicate charges, duplicate messages, or invented recovery numbers.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Recovery_Case_Manager applies a Recovery_Case state transition, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL persist the new state, the incremented Recovery_Case version, and the corresponding Audit_Record within one atomic transaction that commits all three writes or none, and SHALL make the new state readable only after that transaction commits.
2. IF the persistence store rejects a Recovery_Case state transition or does not complete it within PERSISTENCE_TIMEOUT, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL retain the prior state as the readable state, SHALL persist no Audit_Record recording the attempted transition as applied, SHALL issue no Payment_Provider request and no Communication_Provider request for that Recovery_Case, and SHALL report a failure indication naming the Recovery_Case and the rejected transition to the calling component.
3. IF the persistence store is unavailable when Event_Ingestion receives a Payment_Event, THEN THE Event_Ingestion SHALL respond with HTTP status 503, SHALL persist no partial Payment_Event record, SHALL create no Recovery_Case, and SHALL issue no Payment_Provider request and no Communication_Provider request derived from that Payment_Event. **[ASSUMPTION]** the Payment_Provider redelivers on a 5xx response and Revora depends on that redelivery to resend the Payment_Event; redelivery behavior, retry schedule, and redelivery attempt count are **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** pending design-phase verification.
4. WHILE persistence operations return errors or exceed PERSISTENCE_TIMEOUT, THE Revora system SHALL withhold every Payment_Provider request and every Communication_Provider request, SHALL leave every affected Recovery_Case in its last persisted state, and SHALL record no action outcome, recovered amount, executed-action counter change, or customer-message counter change for any withheld action.
5. WHEN a background worker terminates during an execution attempt, THE Execution_Engine SHALL resolve the recorded execution-intent state for the Idempotency_Key of that attempt to CONFIRMED, FAILED, or UNCERTAIN against authoritative provider state before issuing any further external call for the same Recovery_Case, SHALL reuse that same Idempotency_Key rather than deriving a new one for the interrupted attempt, and SHALL leave the executed-action counter and the customer-message counter unchanged until that resolution is persisted.
6. WHEN the Revora system restarts, THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL reload every non-terminal Recovery_Case from persisted records, SHALL re-evaluate Recovery_Window expiry, Cooldown state, the executed-action counter, and the customer-message counter of each reloaded Recovery_Case from those persisted records only, SHALL transition to EXPIRED every reloaded Recovery_Case whose Recovery_Window elapsed during the interruption, and SHALL schedule no action for a reloaded Recovery_Case before that re-evaluation completes for that Recovery_Case.
7. IF two components request a state transition for the same Recovery_Case concurrently, THEN THE Recovery_Case_Manager SHALL commit exactly one transition against the Recovery_Case version read at request time, SHALL reject every remaining request without altering the state written by the committed transition, SHALL write a VERSION_CONFLICT Audit_Record for each rejected request, and SHALL report the rejection to the calling component so that the caller re-reads the current state before issuing any external call.
8. WHEN Event_Ingestion receives a replay of Payment_Events whose provider_event_id values are already persisted, in any order and in any quantity, THE Revora system SHALL create no additional Recovery_Case, SHALL apply no additional Recovery_Case state transition, SHALL issue no additional Payment_Provider request and no additional Communication_Provider request, and SHALL leave the executed-action counter and the customer-message counter of every affected Recovery_Case unchanged.
9. THE Revora system SHALL persist every timestamp used in a timing evaluation as a UTC instant and SHALL evaluate Recovery_Window expiry, Cooldown elapsed, OUTCOME_WAIT_TIMEOUT, and INGEST_ACK_TIMEOUT against those stored UTC timestamps, using no local-time or offset-bearing representation in the comparison.
10. THE Event_Ingestion SHALL parse an accepted Payment_Event payload into a canonical internal Payment_Event representation carrying every field required by downstream components, SHALL produce an identical canonical representation for two payloads that differ only in key order, in insignificant whitespace, or in offset representations of the same instant, and SHALL serialize a canonical internal Payment_Event representation back into a provider-shaped payload.
11. FOR ALL valid Payment_Event payloads, parsing the payload, serializing the parse result, and parsing the serialized output SHALL produce a canonical Payment_Event representation equal to the first parse result in the set of populated fields and in every canonical field value, with equality independent of payload key order and formatting (round-trip property).
12. THE Merchant_Dashboard SHALL present every quarantined malformed Payment_Event for Merchant_User review together with the recorded validation failure and the quarantine timestamp in UTC, and SHALL exclude quarantined Payment_Events from every presented recovery figure.
13. WHEN Event_Ingestion parses an accepted Payment_Event carrying a timestamp expressed with a non-UTC offset, THE Event_Ingestion SHALL convert that timestamp to the equivalent UTC instant before persistence and SHALL retain the received representation alongside the persisted Payment_Event.
14. IF parsing, serializing, and re-parsing a Payment_Event payload produces a canonical Payment_Event representation not equal to the first parse result, THEN THE Event_Ingestion SHALL apply the malformed Payment_Event handling defined in Requirement 1 criterion 6, SHALL create no Recovery_Case from that Payment_Event, and SHALL issue no Payment_Provider request and no Communication_Provider request derived from that Payment_Event.
15. WHEN persistence operations succeed again after an interval of persistence unavailability, THE Revora system SHALL re-evaluate Recovery_Window expiry, Cooldown state, the executed-action counter, and the customer-message counter for every withheld action before issuing that action, and SHALL discard every withheld action whose bounds no longer permit execution, recording the discard as an Audit_Record.

### Requirement 17: Security, Access Control, and Customer Data Protection

**User Story:** As a Merchant, I want payment and customer data protected and every actor identified, so that a recovery system holding sensitive data does not become the largest risk in the stack.

Applicable statutory and card-network obligations are **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** at requirements time. This document states engineering controls only and makes no regulatory compliance claim.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Revora system SHALL require an authenticated Merchant_User session for every Merchant_Dashboard request and every management endpoint request, and SHALL treat a session whose age exceeds SESSION_LIFETIME **[ASSUMPTION]** as unauthenticated.
2. THE Revora system SHALL derive the Merchant identifier from the authenticated Merchant_User session, SHALL ignore any Merchant identifier supplied in the request, and SHALL scope every Recovery_Case read, write, list, and export operation, every Audit_Record query, and every derived metric query to that Merchant identifier.
3. IF an authenticated Merchant_User requests a Recovery_Case, an Audit_Record, or a metric belonging to a different Merchant, THEN THE Revora system SHALL respond with HTTP status 404, SHALL return no field of the requested record, and SHALL write an AUTHORIZATION_DENIED Audit_Record carrying the requesting Merchant_User identifier, the requested record identifier, and the request timestamp.
4. THE Revora system SHALL resolve Payment_Provider credentials, webhook signing secrets, Communication_Provider credentials, and Reasoning_Layer credentials at runtime from configured secret storage held outside source control, SHALL hold no secret value in source-controlled files, in the Audit_Log, or in application logs, and SHALL refuse any external call whose required credential is absent or unreadable, leaving the Recovery_Case state unchanged and writing a CREDENTIAL_UNAVAILABLE Audit_Record.
5. THE Revora system SHALL transmit every request to the Payment_Provider, the Communication_Provider, and the Reasoning_Layer over TLS with server certificate validation enabled.
6. THE Revora system SHALL store customer contact identifiers and payment instrument references in masked or tokenized form at write time, retaining in clear form at most MASK_DISCLOSURE_LENGTH characters of a customer contact identifier and at most MASK_DISCLOSURE_LENGTH characters of a payment instrument reference, and SHALL treat the Payment_Provider as the authoritative holder of payment instrument data.
7. THE Revora system SHALL apply that same masking to customer contact identifiers and payment instrument references, and SHALL omit authentication secrets entirely, before any application log record, error log record, or diagnostic log record is written.
8. WHERE the Reasoning_Layer receives Recovery_Case context, THE Revora system SHALL send only the field set declared in the prompt contract version recorded with the request, excluding payment instrument data, authentication secrets, and raw customer contact identifiers.
9. THE Audit_Log SHALL record in the actor field of every Audit_Record the Merchant_User identifier where a Merchant_User initiated the operation, or the initiating Revora component identifier where no Merchant_User initiated the operation, together with the operation performed and the operation timestamp.
10. WHEN a customer opt-out is recorded, THE Revora system SHALL persist the opt-out status with its source and timestamp as authoritative for every Policy_Engine evaluation beginning after that timestamp, covering every existing Recovery_Case and every subsequently created Recovery_Case of that customer, and SHALL retain the opt-out status until an explicitly recorded customer re-consent replaces it.
11. THE Revora system SHALL delete or irreversibly mask stored customer contact data whose age exceeds CUSTOMER_DATA_RETENTION **[ASSUMPTION]** within 24 hours of that period elapsing, SHALL retain the non-identifying Recovery_Case and Audit_Record fields required for metrics, and SHALL record the retention configuration version applied. Statutory retention floors and ceilings are **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]**.
12. THE Revora system SHALL rate limit the Event_Ingestion endpoint per source identifier to INGEST_RATE_LIMIT **[ASSUMPTION]**, SHALL reject requests exceeding that rate without discarding any already-persisted Payment_Event, and SHALL write a RATE_LIMIT_APPLIED Audit_Record when the configured rate is exceeded.
13. IF a Merchant_Dashboard request or a management endpoint request carries no authenticated Merchant_User session or carries a session older than SESSION_LIFETIME, THEN THE Revora system SHALL reject the request, SHALL return no Recovery_Case field and no Audit_Record field, and SHALL write an AUTHENTICATION_FAILED Audit_Record.
14. IF a connection to the Payment_Provider, the Communication_Provider, or the Reasoning_Layer fails TLS establishment or fails server certificate validation, THEN THE Revora system SHALL abandon the request before transmitting any Recovery_Case field, SHALL leave the Recovery_Case state unchanged, and SHALL write a TRANSPORT_SECURITY_FAILED Audit_Record.
15. IF a Reasoning_Layer request payload contains a field absent from the declared prompt contract field set, THEN THE Revora system SHALL block transmission of the request, SHALL continue on the deterministic fallback path, and SHALL write a PROMPT_CONTRACT_VIOLATION Audit_Record naming the offending field.

### Deferred, Not Silently Included

- Voice agents and multilingual voice interaction.
- Agent swarms, autonomous multi-agent orchestration, and self-directed tool use.
- Kafka, Kubernetes, and a microservice decomposition. Phase 1 is a modular monolith.
- Vector databases and retrieval-augmented generation beyond structured context passing.
- Additional communication channels beyond the single Communication_Provider path.
- Detection triggers other than failed payment: checkout abandonment, missed promise-to-pay, and payment-window expiry are represented in the Payment_Event schema and the Candidate_Action enumeration, and are excluded from Phase 1 detection.
- Automated policy rule learning. Policy changes stay manual and versioned (Requirement 15, criterion 6).
- Automated model retraining without recorded human promotion (Requirement 15, criterion 5).
- Multi-currency valuation. Phase 1 assumes one currency per Merchant. **[ASSUMPTION]**

### Items Requiring Verification Before Design Closes

Each item is **[EVIDENCE INSUFFICIENT]** at requirements time. No API name, endpoint, payload shape, statutory obligation, or industry benchmark has been invented to fill these gaps.

1. Payment_Provider webhook event catalogue, signature scheme, payload fields, delivery guarantees, and redelivery schedule.
2. Payment_Provider recovery action capabilities, including whether payment link creation, payment retry, and payment method update are available under the Merchant account type in use, and their idempotency semantics.
3. Payment_Provider authoritative payment state read capability and its consistency guarantees.
4. Communication_Provider selection, consent model, and delivery confirmation semantics.
5. Real-world baseline recovery rates, intervention recovery rates, action costs, risk costs, and customer costs. Every default in the bounds table is a placeholder.
6. Required sample size per experiment arm for a defensible incremental lift result.
7. Calibration tolerance, diagnosis confidence floor, and minimum segment sample size values.
8. Applicable statutory, contractual, and card-network obligations covering stored customer data and outbound customer contact.
9. Payment_Provider fraud and risk flag availability, the flag values exposed, and their semantics.
10. Permitted masking disclosure length for customer contact identifiers and payment instrument references.
11. Audit retention floor and ceiling applicable to stored Audit_Records.

## Appendix A: Candidate Correctness Properties

These properties are stated for design-phase formalization as property-based tests. Each property names the requirements it derives from. Formal property definitions and generators belong to the design document.

**Policy authority**

- P1: For every executed external action, a Policy_Decision of APPROVED exists in the Audit_Log for that Recovery_Case and Idempotency_Key with a timestamp preceding the execution-intent record. No Reasoning_Layer output alone precedes an execution. (Requirements 4, 8, 9)
- P2: For any Recommendation and any Recovery_Case state, replacing the Reasoning_Layer output with arbitrary schema-valid content leaves the Policy_Decision unchanged. (Requirement 4, criterion 5; Requirement 8, criterion 14)

**Idempotency**

- P3: For any sequence of execution requests carrying one Idempotency_Key, the count of external calls issued is at most one, and every request returns the same recorded result. (Requirement 9, criteria 3 through 6)
- P4: For any inbound Payment_Event sequence containing duplicates and reorderings, the count of Recovery_Cases created for one payment identifier is at most one. (Requirement 1, criteria 4, 7, 9, 10; Requirement 16, criterion 8)

**State machine legality and termination**

- P5: For any sequence of transition requests, the persisted Recovery_Case state history contains only transitions present in the legal transition table. (Requirement 2, criteria 2, 3)
- P6: For any Recovery_Case, the state history reaches a Terminal_State within RECOVERY_WINDOW_DURATION plus OUTCOME_WAIT_TIMEOUT of the detection timestamp, and no state appears after a Terminal_State other than a verified payment reconciliation. (Requirement 2, criteria 4, 12)

**No action after PAID or OPT_OUT**

- P7: For any Recovery_Case whose verified payment state became paid at time T, no customer-visible or payment-affecting action holds a confirmed execution timestamp later than T, except actions already in flight, which are recorded as POST_PAYMENT_ACTION. (Requirement 8, criterion 3; Requirement 10, criteria 4, 5)
- P8: For any customer holding opt-out status, the count of confirmed customer-visible actions across every Recovery_Case of that customer after the opt-out timestamp equals zero. (Requirement 8, criterion 4; Requirement 17, criterion 10)

**Bounds**

- P9: For any Recovery_Case, the confirmed executed-action count is at most MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS and the confirmed customer-message count is at most MAX_CUSTOMER_MESSAGES. (Requirement 2, criteria 7, 8; Requirement 8, criteria 6, 7)
- P10: For any two consecutive confirmed outbound actions on one Recovery_Case, the interval between them is at least COOLDOWN_INTERVAL. (Requirement 8, criterion 8)
- P11: For any confirmed action, the execution timestamp falls inside the Recovery_Window of the Recovery_Case. (Requirement 2, criterion 6; Requirement 8, criterion 9)

**Audit trail**

- P12: For any Recovery_Case, Audit_Record sequence numbers are strictly increasing, contain no gaps, and no persisted Audit_Record is modified or removed by any operation. (Requirement 11, criteria 3, 4, 9)
- P13: For any inbound Payment_Event, every Audit_Record produced from that Payment_Event carries the same correlation_id. (Requirement 11, criterion 7)

**Incremental value arithmetic**

- P14: For any Candidate_Action set, incremental_probability equals intervention_recovery_probability minus baseline_recovery_probability, expected_incremental_revenue equals payment_amount multiplied by incremental_probability, and net_recovery_value equals expected_incremental_revenue minus the three cost terms, computed in integer minor units. (Requirement 7, criteria 1 through 3, 12)
- P15: For any Candidate_Action set, the selected Candidate_Action holds a net_recovery_value greater than or equal to the net_recovery_value of every other Candidate_Action not excluded by policy or by an exclusion rule. (Requirement 7, criterion 4)
- P16: For any Candidate_Action set where every intervention net_recovery_value falls below MIN_NET_VALUE_THRESHOLD, the selection is DO_NOTHING or WAIT. (Requirement 7, criterion 5)
- P17: For any Candidate_Action set where baseline_recovery_probability is greater than or equal to HIGH_BASELINE_THRESHOLD and no Candidate_Action clears both thresholds, the selection is DO_NOTHING or WAIT. This is the "customer was going to pay anyway" property. (Requirement 7, criterion 6)
- P18: The Candidate_Action holding the greatest intervention_recovery_probability is not necessarily the selection; whenever the two differ, both appear in the Recommendation with their net_recovery_value figures. (Requirement 7, criterion 8)
- P19: DO_NOTHING always holds incremental_probability equal to zero, action_cost equal to zero, and therefore net_recovery_value equal to zero minus risk_cost. (Requirement 6, criterion 4)

**Metrics and causality**

- P20: For any reporting period, observed_recovered_revenue plus natural_recovered_revenue equals the sum of confirmed recovered amounts, each recovered amount is counted once, and unresolved_revenue equals the summed payment_amount of non-recovered Terminal_State Recovery_Cases. (Requirement 10, criteria 8, 9, 10, 11; Requirement 12, criteria 1 through 3, 5)
- P21: incremental_recovered_revenue carries a numeric value only where a completed experiment reports an uncertainty interval excluding zero; otherwise incremental_recovered_revenue is reported NOT_ESTABLISHED and the reported observed recovery carries the label CAUSALITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED. (Requirement 12, criteria 4, 9, 13; Requirement 13, criteria 8, 9)
- P22: For any two consecutive periods where recovery_rate increases and net_recovered_revenue decreases, a COST_OUTPACING_RECOVERY finding exists. (Requirement 12, criterion 8)
- P23: For any metric derived from a Synthetic_Dataset, the provenance label SYNTHETIC appears in every presentation of that metric. (Requirement 12, criterion 11; Requirement 14, criterion 9)

**Experiment integrity**

- P24: For one experiment identifier and one Recovery_Case identifier, group assignment is deterministic and stable across repeated evaluation, and the assignment timestamp precedes the first Diagnosis record. (Requirement 13, criteria 1, 2)
- P25: For any Recovery_Case assigned to Control_Group, the count of Revora-selected interventions executed equals zero. (Requirement 13, criterion 3)
- P26: For any experiment labelled INVALIDATED or holding a Recovery_Case labelled CONTAMINATED, the count of Recovery_Cases in that experiment classified Attributed_Recovery equals zero, and every CONTAMINATED Recovery_Case is absent from every reported group result. (Requirement 13, criteria 8, 15, 16)

**Event canonicalization**

- P27: For any valid Payment_Event payload, parsing the payload, serializing the parse result, and parsing the serialized output produces a canonical representation equal to the first parse result in populated field set and in every canonical field value, with equality holding across payloads differing only in key order, insignificant whitespace, or offset representation of the same instant; any payload for which that equality fails produces no Recovery_Case and no external request. (Requirement 16, criteria 10, 11, 14)

**Decision-cycle bounds**

- P28: For any Recovery_Case, the recorded decision-cycle count is at most MAX_RECOVERY_ATTEMPTS, and any transition into DECISION_PENDING requested at that count leaves the Recovery_Case in STOPPED rather than raising the count. (Requirement 2, criteria 7, 14)

**Audit-write blocking**

- P29: For any Recovery_Case whose Policy_Decision Audit_Record or executed-action Audit_Record failed to persist, the count of external actions executed for that Recovery_Case after that failure and before that Audit_Record persists equals zero. (Requirement 11, criterion 10)

**Tenant isolation**

- P30: For any authenticated Merchant_User, every Recovery_Case, Audit_Record, and metric returned by any read, list, query, or export operation belongs to the Merchant derived from that Merchant_User's session, and any request naming a record of another Merchant returns no field of that record. (Requirement 17, criteria 2, 3)
